About Sarthak Solar Solutions

Professional solar energy solutions for homes, businesses and commercial establishments — making clean, reliable and cost-effective solar energy accessible to all.

Who We Are

Sarthak Solar Solutions is a professional solar energy solutions company dedicated to helping households, businesses, and commercial establishments adopt clean, reliable, and cost-effective solar energy.

Our focus is to make rooftop solar simple and accessible for customers who want to reduce their electricity bills and become more energy independent.

We provide complete solar solutions, including solar system consultation, site assessment, system planning, installation, maintenance, inspection, repair and after-sales support.

We also assist eligible customers in understanding and completing the process related to the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, including guidance regarding available government subsidy benefits and the required solar installation process.

Why Choose Sarthak Solar Solutions?

We believe that solar installation is not just about putting panels on a roof. A properly planned solar system should match the customer's electricity consumption, available rooftop space, budget and future requirements.

Our team focuses on providing transparent guidance, quality installation practices and dependable service so that customers can confidently make the transition towards solar energy.

Our goal is simple — reduce electricity expenses, promote clean energy and help more homes and businesses become energy independent.
